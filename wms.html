Warehouse Management System

Real-time inventory control and warehouse operations, built for distributors handling perishable and general goods alike.

Inventory Control

  • Quantity on hand, reserved and available per location
  • Batch / lot tracking from receiving to dispatch
  • Expiry date monitoring with near-expiry alerts
  • Stock reservation on order confirmation
  • Manual adjustments with reason codes
  • Stock transfer between locations or bins

Warehouse Operations

  • Goods receiving against purchase orders
  • Quality inspection — passed or rejected
  • Put-away to warehouse bins
  • Pick lists generated per order, by bin location
  • Packing confirmation and packing list
  • Dispatch linked to delivery manifest

Alerts

  • Low stock — quantity at or below reorder point
  • Out of stock — quantity available reaches zero
  • Near expiry — configurable threshold, default 3 days

Hardware Compatibility

Works with standard barcode scanners and thermal label printers used in warehouse environments. Talk to us about your existing hardware during setup.
