Logistics Management System

Delivery planning, execution and live tracking — including home delivery and self-collection points.

Delivery Planning

  • Daily delivery schedules
  • Route planning, grouped by area
  • Vehicle assignment to manifest
  • Driver assignment to manifest

Delivery Execution

  • Digital delivery manifest — stops and items
  • Live GPS location shared by driver app
  • Customer signature capture
  • Delivery photo evidence
  • Electronic proof of delivery (ePOD) per stop

Shopee-Style Delivery Status

  • Pending — order confirmed, awaiting packing
  • Packed — ready for dispatch
  • Out for delivery — live map tracking active
  • Delivered — all items delivered
  • Failed — delivery attempt failed

Delivery Options

Supports both home delivery to a customer address and self-collection from configured pickup points, with distance-based sorting at checkout.
