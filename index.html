About Us

Order4U Cloud is an AI-native software company based in Sarawak, Malaysia. We design and build warehouse and logistics management systems with our in-house development team, and also work as a system integrator — helping businesses digitise their operations end to end.

Frequently Asked Questions

A few things prospects usually ask us.

How long does implementation take?

Timeline depends on your business size and data complexity — we scope a clear plan together during your consultation.

Can staff use it without constant internet access?

Our apps are designed mobile-first for warehouse and driver use. If you have specific offline requirements, we'll scope them with you.

What hardware do we need?

Standard barcode scanners and thermal label printers are supported — we advise on compatible hardware during setup.

Do you provide training and support?

Yes — onboarding includes staff training, with ongoing support available after go-live.
